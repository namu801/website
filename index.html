01  Profile

About

Yoonji Nam
Product Designer

I design products where the hardware, the software, and the person using them all have to agree.

Most of my work has been in mobility: passenger infotainment across three in-cabin displays, control apps for drivers and passengers, and the operator tool that keeps them supplied with content. 02  Approach

How I Work

Start from the problem, not the screen

I read usage metrics alongside interviews and field observation before deciding what to draw. On the parking kiosk project that meant 37 machines of data and five sites of watching people fail at them — the redesign came out of what they did, not what the spec said.

Design the system, not one surface

A passenger display is only convincing if the control pad and the operator tool behave consistently with it. I map the whole journey across hardware and software first, then design each surface to hold its part of it.

Ship it with engineers

I own the information architecture and the design system, keep the handoff current, and stay in the build. On the PBV content management system that meant carrying it through Alpha launch and GPS-linked road testing rather than handing off a file.

Tie the work to something measurable

Redesigning the docent app voting flow moved satisfaction 18pp to 98% and participation to 91.4%. I frame decisions around outcomes like these — participation, brand experience, operational efficiency — so the design has a reason beyond preference.
